A privateer carried a letter of marque — written authorization to do what would otherwise be
piracy. That is exactly the footing of this track. The passive track (Seamanship) never transmits;
this one does, so it exists separately and only ever under authorization: your own network, or a
documented engagement.

It starts with a Wi-Fi passphrase audit and will grow as more authorized-only material is added.
The rule never changes with it — own gear or written permission, nothing else — and the platform
enforces it: each module's lab stays locked until its legal quiz is passed, and asks you to attest
the target is in scope before it will run.
