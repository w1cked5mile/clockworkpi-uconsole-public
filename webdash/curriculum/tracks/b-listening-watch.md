A listening watch is the hands on deck whose only job is to hear what is out there. This track turns
you from someone who can run the radios into someone who can *read* them: how far a signal should
carry (a link budget), how to take a clean, disciplined **SDR** (software-defined radio) capture, and
how to name a mode from its **waterfall** — the scrolling spectrogram display. From there it extends
to the land-mobile and maritime bands, the passive Wi-Fi and Bluetooth survey, satellite passes, and
a field capstone.

**Do Track A first.** This track includes and builds on Track A's foundation — power, the rules,
position, the mesh and the sky overhead — so start there if you haven't.

**Intended sequence** (dependency-ordered): the Track A foundation → **M2 · dB, antennas and link
budget** → **M5 · SDR foundations and capture discipline** → **M8 · Identifying signals** →
**M12 · Land-mobile and maritime** → **M7 · Passive Wi-Fi and Bluetooth survey** → **M9 · Satellite
passes** → **M11 · Capstone field session**. Every module ends with a lab you run on Fancy, checked
against live readings.

**Available today** (~23 h): the Track A modules, **M2 · dB, antennas and link budget**,
**M5 · SDR foundations and capture discipline**, **M8 · Identifying signals**, **M12 · Land-mobile
and maritime**, **all of M7 · Passive Wi-Fi and Bluetooth survey** (the AC1200 adapter is fitted, so
both the Wi-Fi half at 2.4/5/6 GHz and the passive-**Bluetooth** half run), and **M9 · Satellite
passes** (its planning half always; its live ISS capture when a good pass and an active station line
up). The **M2 → M5 → M8 → M12** analysis spine is built end to end through the survey and on to the
sky, and the capstone **M11 · Capstone field session** closes the track — its lessons (composing a
power budget, designing the session) are ready now, while its ~2 h battery lab (LAB-20) is
**power-gated** and stays locked until the battery pack is verified (the interim 18650 pair is
unverified and JP1 is open). The whole track is now built; the only thing waiting is that hardware
gate on the final lab.
