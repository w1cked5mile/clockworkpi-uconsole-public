---

term: With position
tooltip: Aircraft that have sent both halves of a CPR position.
good_bad: Always ≤ the aircraft count; a new aircraft gains a position a few seconds after it appears.
try_this: Watch a new aircraft appear in the count before it gets a position.
learn_more: '#/learn/m/M6'
---

Position needs an even and an odd CPR message within about 10 s; after that, single messages keep it updated.
