---
id: eph
term: Error estimate (eph)
tooltip: gpsd's estimate of horizontal position error, in metres, about 95 % confidence — an estimate, not a measurement.
good_bad: Fancy has shown about 17–21 m with a good fix.
try_this: Compare eph with HDOP over a few minutes.
learn_more: '#/learn/m/M3'
---

eph comes from the receiver's own error model combined with geometry. It is not the difference from your true position.
