---
id: bssid
term: BSSID
tooltip: The hardware address of one access point's radio — different from the network name (SSID).
good_bad: "One SSID can have many BSSIDs (several access points, or several bands). BSSIDs never go in a finding."
try_this: "In Kismet, find one network name that appears with more than one BSSID."
learn_more: "#/learn/m/M7"
---

The SSID is the name people choose; the BSSID is the address of the radio broadcasting it. A
mesh system or a dual-band router shows one SSID and several BSSIDs, so counting BSSIDs counts
radios, not networks. Both are identifiers of someone's equipment and stay out of findings.
