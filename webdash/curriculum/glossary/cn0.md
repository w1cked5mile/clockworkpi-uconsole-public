---
id: cn0
term: C/N0 (dB-Hz)
tooltip: A satellite's signal strength as carrier-to-noise density, in dB-Hz.
good_bad: Above 40 strong, 30–40 good, 20–30 weak but trackable. In a 9-satellite fix, Fancy's satellites ranged 15–33.
try_this: Hold the GPS near a window and watch C/N0 in PyGPSClient rise.
learn_more: '#/learn/m/M3'
---

Measured against the noise in 1 Hz of bandwidth, so it is not comparable to a LoRa SNR in dB.
