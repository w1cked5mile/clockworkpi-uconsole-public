---
id: ttff
term: Time to first fix (TTFF)
tooltip: How long the receiver takes from power-on to its first valid fix.
good_bad: Seconds when it has recent orbit data; minutes from cold; maybe never indoors.
try_this: LAB-04 times it after a GPS rail cycle.
learn_more: '#/learn/m/M3'
---

Depends on what the receiver already knows: time, position, almanac and ephemeris.
