---
id: pdop
term: PDOP
tooltip: Position dilution of precision — satellite geometry for the 3D position (horizontal and vertical).
good_bad: Under about 3 is good; it is always ≥ HDOP.
try_this: Watch PDOP and HDOP change together as satellites rise and set.
learn_more: '#/learn/m/M3'
---

PDOP combines HDOP and VDOP. Like HDOP it is geometry only, not signal strength.
