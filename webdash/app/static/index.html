      Listen

      Live audio from one channel, or the airband scanner, which cycles channels and pauses on traffic. Starting a new listen takes the tuner from the current one.

      Cycles airband channels and pauses on traffic. Run an airband sweep on the Hunt tab first to scan just the live ones; otherwise it covers 118–137 MHz (slower). Press Stop to end.

      Receiver — OpenWebRX

      A web-native SDR receiver: a live waterfall and tunable demodulators in your browser. It owns the tuner the whole time it runs, so ADS-B, Hunt and Listen are blocked until you stop it. Receive-only. It has its own admin login (set once on the device).

      Surveys & reports

      NameWhenWPsReports
      —

      Packet capture — passive troubleshooting

      Captures Fancy's own traffic to a bounded ring buffer (~100 MB cap in ~/labs/tshark) to help diagnose hangs and dropouts. It only listens — there is no transmit path. The dashboard shows counts only; the saved .pcapng (which does contain addresses) stays on the device for a Wireshark deep-dive.

      WPA audit — authorized equipment only 🔒

      Active auditing (deauth + handshake capture) runs only against a BSSID on the allowlist below — equipment you own or have written authorization to test. Adding a BSSID here is your authorization assertion; the host enforces it.

      BSSIDSSIDBasisAdded
      —

      Capture handshake

      Sends a few bounded, targeted deauth frames at the selected authorized AP to force a handshake, captures it, and reports the result. It needs the USB rail on and puts the AC1200 (wlan1) into monitor mode. Kismet is paused during capture and restored after.

      Offload the captured handshake to the GPU host (gpu-host) to recover the passphrase. Runs over the tailnet; the host must be online. The recovered key is shown here and not stored.
