WPA audit — authorized equipment only

Active auditing (deauth + handshake capture) runs only against a BSSID on the allowlist below — equipment you own or have written authorization to test. Adding a BSSID here is your authorization assertion; the host enforces it.

Capture handshake

Sends a few bounded, targeted deauth frames at the selected authorized AP to force a handshake, captures it, and reports the result. Kismet is paused during capture and restored after.

Offload the captured handshake to the GPU host (gpu-host) to recover the passphrase. Runs over the tailnet; the host must be online. The recovered key is shown here and not stored.

Packet capture — passive troubleshooting

Captures Fancy's own traffic to a bounded ring buffer (~100 MB cap in ~/labs/tshark) to help diagnose hangs and dropouts. It only listens — there is no transmit path. The dashboard shows counts only; the saved .pcapng (which does contain addresses) stays on the device for a Wireshark deep-dive.
