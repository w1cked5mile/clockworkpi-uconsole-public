## Convention

Photograph every item as received, before assembly — it is the evidence that settles a warranty or
RMA dispute. Keep originals; commit downscaled copies so the repository stays readable on a slow
connection.

Anything read off a photograph is a **photo reading, not a measurement**. Silkscreen revisions, part
counts, and connector types taken from these frames are marked *unverified* wherever they are used
and must be confirmed with the part in hand before they drive an assembly step or a purchase.
