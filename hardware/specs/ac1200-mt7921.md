## Why it matters here

**Role: all recon, on both radios** (owner decision, 2026-09-25). The AC1200's Wi-Fi (`wlan1`) is the
survey adapter and its own Bluetooth controller (`hci1`) is the passive-BLE controller. The onboard
combo chip is for Fancy's own use — `wlan0` for its network link, `hci0` for its paired Bluetooth
devices — and is never used for recon. This card is what unblocks the monitor-mode discipline: the
CM4's onboard Wi-Fi (`brcmfmac`, `wlan0`) **has no monitor mode** (verified 2026-09-21, 2026-09-25,
and again 2026-09-30), and the Ralink RT5370 that stood in (2.4 GHz only, no Bluetooth) is now
removed — it no longer appears in `lsusb`, and `wlan1` is the MT7921.

Its 4 IPEX leads land on `ANT2`/`ANT3` (Bluetooth, the `BT0`/`BT1` connectors) and `ANT5`/`ANT6`
(Wi-Fi) — the four antennas that had no radio behind them until this arrived. Every workflow in
[`../../knowledge/wardriving/`](../../knowledge/wardriving/README.md) depends on a monitor-capable
adapter, so this was the gate on that discipline.

## Verified passive-BLE behaviour on `hci1` (2026-09-30)

The passive-only guarantee holds on the AC1200's controller, confirmed with `btmon` on Fancy:
- **No bonds** on `hci1` (none anywhere on Fancy).
- **`bluetoothd` does not auto-scan or auto-connect it.** On bring-up (`hciconfig hci1 reset` under
  `btmon`) it issued only setup commands — **no `LE Set Scan Enable`, no `Create Connection`** — and
  it cleared the accept and resolving lists; `Scanning: Enabled` never appeared while idle.
- **A passive scan is allowed** — `hcitool -i hci1 lescan --passive` set `Type: Passive (0x00)` and
  ran with **no `Command Disallowed`**; 304 advertising reports from 16 advertisers were captured.
  Afterwards `hci1` returned to `bluetoothd`, idle, with no bonds created.
- **Gotcha:** with `bluetoothd` running, `hcitool … lescan --passive` fails with **"Broken pipe"**
  (socket contention, *not* a controller rejection). Stop `bluetoothd` (or pause its scan) first —
  safe here because `hci0` has no active connections or bonds. See
  [`../../knowledge/wardriving/learned/ble-passive-observation.md`](../../knowledge/wardriving/learned/ble-passive-observation.md).
- Pair nothing to this controller — a bond would bring back `bluetoothd`'s background scan.

## Driver / bring-up notes

- `mt7921u` (USB) is mainline; Bluetooth on the same chip uses `btmtk` firmware — both loaded cleanly
  on 2026-09-30.
- Re-checks after any replug: `dmesg | grep -i mt79`, `iw dev` (interface name + MAC),
  `iw phy | grep -A14 'Supported interface modes'` (monitor), `hciconfig -a` (hci index + bus),
  `iw reg get` (regulatory domain).
- Turn the USB rail on first (`aiov2_ctl USB on`); with it off the port has no power and the card does
  not enumerate.
